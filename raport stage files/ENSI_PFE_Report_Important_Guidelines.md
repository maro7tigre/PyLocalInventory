# ENSI — Important Guidelines for the PFE Report

## 1. Page de garde
- The **cover page must strictly follow the ENSI model** provided in the official document.
- It should include the PFE title, student information, jury information, date of defense, and academic year as required by the model.

## 2. Maximum length
- Maximum report length: **60 pages**, **excluding annexes**.

## 3. Formatting
- **Line spacing:** 1.15
- **Margins:**
  - Left: 2 cm
  - Right: 2 cm
  - Top: 1.5 cm
  - Bottom: 1.5 cm
  - Footer: 1 cm
- **Chapter titles:** 16 pt
- **Paragraph titles:** 14 pt
- **Subparagraph titles and body text:** 12 pt
- **Body text:** 12 pt and **justified** (left and right).

## 4. Page numbering
- Preliminary pages: **Roman numerals** — i, ii, iii, ...
- Main report: **Arabic numerals** — 1, 2, 3, ...

## 5. Required report structure
The document states that the following sections must appear **in this order**:

1. Page de garde
2. Dédicace
3. Remerciements
4. Résumé en français + mots clés
5. Abstract en anglais + keywords
6. Table des matières
7. Liste des figures
8. Liste des tableaux
9. Liste des abréviations
10. Introduction générale
11. Chapitre 1 — Contexte et état de l’art
12. Chapitre 2 — Méthodologie / conception
13. Chapitre 3 — Réalisation et résultats
14. Conclusion générale et perspectives
15. Références bibliographiques
16. Annexes

## 6. Standard chapter structure
Each chapter should follow a structure such as:

- **Chapitre X : Titre**
- **I.X — Introduction**
- **I.X — Paragraphe**
- **I.X.X — Sous-paragraphe**
- **I.X.X.X — Sous-paragraphe**
- **I.X — Conclusion**

The exact numbering depends on the chapter and number of sections.

## 7. Introduction générale
The general introduction should:
- Present the concept/topic of the PFE, its emergence, evolution, and current state.
- Present the **problematic/research problem** and place it in its study context.
- Explain the **overall approach** used to answer the problem, including justification of the major steps taken.
- Announce the **overall structure/plan of the PFE**.

## 8. Chapter and section introductions/conclusions
### Introductions
Introductions to parts and chapters should:
- Position the chapter/part within the overall PFE plan.
- Announce its objective/final purpose.
- Explain the approach followed.
- Introduce specific definitions or technical terms when necessary.

### Conclusions
Conclusions of parts and chapters should:
- Summarize the main elements and lessons learned.
- Prepare/announce the following part or chapter.

## 9. Conclusion générale
The general conclusion should directly answer the problem stated in the introduction.

It should:
- Briefly recall the concept, problematic, and study context.
- Summarize the **main results**, recommendations, added value, impact, and main lessons learned.
- Present the **limitations** of the work and possible improvements.
- Present possible **future directions/openings** by extending the problem or applying the work elsewhere.

## 10. Equations
- **Every equation, mathematical expression, or formula must be numbered.**
- Example format: `(1)`

## 11. Figures and tables
### Figures / curves
- Must be **numbered**.
- Must have a **title placed after the figure/curve**.
- Must be explicitly mentioned in the body of the text.
- Must be **centered and readable**.

Example:
> Figure 2.3 : Architecture du système

### Tables
- Must be **centered and numbered**.
- Must have a **title placed before the table**.
- Must be explicitly mentioned in the text.

Example:
> Tableau 3.1 : Comparaison des performances

## 12. Bibliographic references and webography
- References and websites consulted must be **cited in the text**.
- They must be presented at the end of the report according to the applicable standards.
- The document recommends using **Word's automatic bibliography/reference function**.
- For websites, include the **date of last consultation**, because web content can change.

## 13. Annexes
- Annexes contain external material that is important for understanding the PFE.
- They should provide additional information that helps the reader understand or deepen the work.
- Annexes must be inserted **in the order in which they are cited in the report**.
- They must not be added randomly.
- They should be numbered: **Annexe A, Annexe B, ...**

## 14. Company / organization presentation
- The presentation of the company, organization, or laboratory where the PFE was completed must **not exceed 4 pages**.
- For the oral defense, this presentation should be limited to **1–2 slides**.

## 15. Printing and digital version
- The printed report must be printed **recto only** (single-sided).
- A **PDF digital version is mandatory**.

## 16. Plagiarism
- Any material borrowed from a source must be **properly cited**.
- Plagiarism can result in **rejection of the report or an academic sanction**.

## 17. Most important checklist before submission
- [ ] Cover page follows the ENSI model exactly.
- [ ] Report is ≤ 60 pages excluding annexes.
- [ ] Correct margins and 1.15 line spacing.
- [ ] Correct font sizes for chapters, sections, and body text.
- [ ] Body text is justified.
- [ ] Preliminary pages use Roman numerals.
- [ ] Main report uses Arabic page numbers.
- [ ] All mandatory sections are present and in the required order.
- [ ] Introduction explains context, problem, approach, and plan.
- [ ] Chapters contain introductions and conclusions.
- [ ] All equations are numbered.
- [ ] Figures are numbered, centered, readable, titled below, and referenced in the text.
- [ ] Tables are numbered, centered, titled above, and referenced in the text.
- [ ] References are cited in the text and listed correctly.
- [ ] Web sources include consultation dates.
- [ ] Annexes are numbered and ordered by citation.
- [ ] Company presentation is ≤ 4 pages.
- [ ] Printed version is single-sided.
- [ ] PDF version is prepared.
- [ ] No plagiarism; all borrowed material is cited.
